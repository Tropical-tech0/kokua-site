Kokua Português

All your events in one place

Kokua is your events showcase: concerts, theatre, cinema, exhibitions, sport, activities for children, talks and much more — organised by category, date and city.

The app for Android and iOS is in preparation.

The app

What you can do

Discover

See on the map or in a list what is on near you, today, this weekend or in the coming days.

Go to the source

Each event links to its official page, where you can check the details and buy tickets.

Take part

Mark the events you are going to, set reminders and rate the ones you attended.

Create events

Publish your own events. Our team reviews each one before it appears in the app.

Free and without ads

Kokua is free for people looking for events and has no ads. Event organisers can choose a plan to publish more. Your account lets you mark events you are going to, rate them and create events — we do not sell your data.

Where do the events come from?

Every day we gather the public listings of cultural organisations and venues. Each event always links to its official page, where you can check the details and buy tickets. Users can also create events, which our team reviews before they are published.

We started with Portugal

We are adding new cities and countries.

For organisations and venues

We list public programming with a link to your official page. If you represent an organisation and want to correct something, add your listings or have your events removed from Kokua, write to us: we will reply and deal with your request.
